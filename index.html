About me

JB

I came to technical writing from the other side of documentation: implementing software for customers. As a solution consultant at Kinaxis and a solution engineer at Whatfix, I configured and tested enterprise software, trained clients, and wrote the guides that teams relied on after go-live. That work taught me what people need from documentation when something isn't working.

Along the way, I completed a 700-hour full-stack Java bootcamp, so I'm comfortable reading code, running commands, and testing every step I write. Today at Pluralsight, I review and edit hands-on labs and courses, and I help authors turn expert knowledge into instructions that learners can follow.

I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I'm open to relocation.

How I work

I test what I write

I run every lab step and code sample myself, reproduce reported errors, and confirm the fix in the platform before content goes out.

I write to a standard

I edit to the Google Developer Documentation Style Guide and internal content standards, and I keep process documentation current as tools change.

I work closely with experts

I give authors and subject matter experts specific, actionable feedback and coordinate reviews and deadlines through publication.
